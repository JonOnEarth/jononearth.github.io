◎ There’s a research question hiding in here. +

What do we know—and what will our teammates learn next?

Every rover sees a small part of the same world. Sharing discoveries makes that picture more complete. Sharing plans can help a team avoid spending its next move on the same place.

This is a small exploration puzzle inspired by my work on multi-agent decisions. Coverage counts each observed tile once, even when several rovers see it. The game uses exact tile observations and free communication; it does not implement the paper’s probabilistic inference or reproduce its experiments. Private-map mode shows one rover’s knowledge at a time; you can switch rovers and remember what you saw.
