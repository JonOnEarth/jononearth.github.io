About me

I’m Peng Wu (吴鹏), an AI researcher and builder based in Boston. I work on trustworthy AI: how distributed systems can learn from incomplete information and make decisions safely, with privacy, security, and reliability in mind.

My path began in physics and optical engineering. I completed my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering at Northeastern University in 2024, advised by Pau Closas. My doctoral work on Bayesian data fusion for distributed learning explored how to combine information from different sources while accounting for uncertainty and shared knowledge.

Today, I’m a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Northeastern, working with Mahdi Imani on distributed learning, safety in mixed reality, and multi-agent decisions. I’m also Chief Scientist at AgentMark.ai, where I build AI agents for advertising.
