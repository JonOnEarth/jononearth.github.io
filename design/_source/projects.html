Inside the work

The problem

Advertising work connects strategy, data, content, and repeated operational tasks. Useful automation needs to bring those pieces together.

My contribution

I design and build the AI agent, connecting LLM capabilities with campaign data and advertising tools. This includes the conversational interface and its integration into advertising workflows.

The focus

Turning language and data into useful actions: analyzing information, managing advertising workflows, generating content, and supporting automation.

Research applications

02 / Research application

Resilient sensing
& navigation

Applying deep and federated learning to GNSS interference detection and WiFi indoor localization. This work explores learning across heterogeneous data while reducing the need to pool raw observations.

The trustworthy-AI connection

Receivers and users see different conditions. Personalization helps address those differences, while distributed training changes what information needs to leave a device. I also investigated model quantization to reduce communication costs.

Modeling the person
inside the system

Connecting system features, physiological responses, and cybersickness through Bayesian networks. Probabilistic verification checks specified risk properties within the encoded model and evidence.

The trustworthy-AI connection

Prediction accuracy alone does not answer whether a specified risk is acceptable under a given condition. I connect human-state modeling with explicit safety queries, alongside research on engagement estimation under cognitive attack.
